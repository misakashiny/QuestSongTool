Quest Song Tool desktop distribution

This executable bundles Python, PySide6/Qt, Pillow, miniaudio/CFFI and Android
platform-tools. Their original license terms and copyright notices apply.
PyInstaller's hooks collect package license files where provided by the installed
distributions. Android platform-tools NOTICE.txt is included under platform-tools/.
